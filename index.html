FAQ

Your questions.Our answers.

Getting started

What can Code&Craft help me create?⌄

We work across software development, digital design, 3D design and printing, electronics, and rapid prototyping. We can help with one part of a project or bring the disciplines together.

How do I start a project?⌄

Choose a starting point above or open our project inquiry form. Share your idea, requirements, and any useful files. We review the scope with you before the build begins.

Scope & pricing

How much will my project cost?⌄

Each project is quoted for its scope. Software complexity, design requirements, print material and machine time, hardware components, and revisions can affect the estimate.

Can I combine software and hardware?⌄

Yes. We can scope an integrated build that combines an enclosure, electronics, firmware, and a digital interface. The agreed quote specifies what is included.

Is this a checkout?⌄

Your selections prepare a project inquiry. No subscription or payment is processed on this page; project terms are agreed after reviewing the scope.

Design & delivery

Will I be able to review the design?⌄

Our process includes reviewing the design, model, schematic, or proposal with you before proceeding into the agreed build.

How soon can you deliver?⌄

Delivery depends on the work, complexity, materials, and review cycles. We discuss and agree on a timeline after reviewing your requirements.

What will I receive at handover?⌄

The final handover follows the agreed scope: for example, project files, visual assets, software, printed parts, assembled hardware, or documentation.

Support & next steps

Can you improve an existing project?⌄

Share the current files, system, or prototype and what you want to change. We will review feasibility and define an appropriate scope.

Can I request support after delivery?⌄

Tell us the improvements or support you need. We can discuss maintenance, iteration, or further development as a separate or ongoing scope.
